# VANTA / FORM 01 — approved design

Fictional premium unisex fashion sneaker launch, inspired by the editorial product storytelling of the supplied TikTok. Approved for continuous execution by the user ("oke chạy").

## Visual contract
- Charcoal #151515, chalk #F2EDE4, orange #F26935.
- Oversized Vietnamese sans-serif typography; product imagery overlaps the hero type.
- Hero: ĐỊNH CHẤT RIÊNG. Design story: KHÁC BIỆT TỪ MỌI GÓC NHÌN. Lookbook: XUỐNG PHỐ. THEO CÁCH CỦA BẠN. Details, three colorways, demo launch signup, footer.
- FORM 01: low-top, panelled upper, layered sole, orange heel. Chalk, Carbon, Clay colorways.
- One authored Blender master supplies product renders and GLB. Generated imagery is concept/editorial material and must not masquerade as photographs of an actual manufactured shoe.

## Interaction contract
Anchor navigation, responsive menu, colorway buttons with selected state, on-demand 3D viewer with poster/error fallback, desktop scroll-driven product presentation, reduced motion support. Signup validates email locally and explicitly confirms demo only; no data is sent or stored. No commerce, invented performance claims, reviews or real availability.
